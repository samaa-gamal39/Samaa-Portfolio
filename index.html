Featured Case Study — Cleopatra Hospitals Group

Cleopatra Hospitals Group is Egypt's largest private hospital network — incorporated in 1979, consolidated into a single group in 2014, and listed on the Egyptian Exchange since 2016. As team lead, Samaa directed a 10-member group through a full analysis of the company's consolidated income statement and balance sheet for financial years 2024 and 2025: horizontal and vertical analysis of every line item, followed by liquidity, activity, solvency, and profitability ratios, submitted as a discussion-committee-reviewed seminar paper.

KEY FINDINGS · FY2024 → FY2025
Net revenue growth +33.33%
Gross profit margin 37.14% → 39.55%
Net profit margin 15.07% → 13.39%
Earnings per share growth +17.95%
Current ratio 1.06 → 0.71
Quick ratio 0.92 → 0.63
Debt-to-assets ratio 58.13% → 60.5%
Receivables turnover 1.08×
Inventory turnover 14.52×
Return on assets 10.76% → 9.45%

The read: revenue and margins both grew, and shareholders earned more per share — but the group's debt-funded expansion outpaced its ability to collect cash. A current ratio that dropped below 1 and a receivables turnover under 1.1× signalled a real liquidity strain sitting underneath the growth story. The team's recommendation was threefold: speed up collection on credit sales, tighten administrative and financing costs, and rebalance internal versus external financing to bring down the overall cost of capital without losing managerial independence.
